Our Story

Driving Excellence Since 2009

AutoElite was founded with a single mission: to make buying a premium pre-owned vehicle as transparent, enjoyable, and stress-free as possible. Over 15 years, we've helped more than 12,000 customers find their perfect car.

We believe you deserve to know exactly what you're buying. That's why every vehicle in our inventory comes with a comprehensive CARFAX report, our rigorous 150-point inspection, and a no-questions-asked 7-day return policy.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Our values guide every interaction, every inspection, and every sale.
