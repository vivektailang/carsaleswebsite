FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer financing? +
Yes! We work with multiple lenders to offer competitive financing options for all credit profiles. You can get pre-approved online in minutes or speak with our finance team in person. We offer rates starting as low as 3.9% APR for qualified buyers.
What does the 7-day return policy cover? +
If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you can return the vehicle within 7 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the car is in the same condition as when you bought it and has been driven fewer than 500 additional miles.
Can I trade in my current vehicle? +
Absolutely! We accept trade-ins for all makes and models. You can get an instant online estimate or bring your vehicle in for a free appraisal. We'll apply the trade-in value directly to your purchase, making the process seamless.
Do you offer vehicle delivery? +
Yes! We offer free delivery within 100 miles of our dealership. For deliveries beyond 100 miles, a flat fee applies. Contact us for details and to arrange delivery to your location.
What warranty comes with certified pre-owned vehicles? +
All AutoElite Certified vehicles come with a 12-month / 12,000-mile limited powertrain warranty. Extended warranty plans are also available for purchase. Each certified vehicle has passed our rigorous 150-point inspection.
